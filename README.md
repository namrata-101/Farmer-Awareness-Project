# Farmer-Awareness-Project
A collaborative project that researches government schemes for farmers, collects survey data, and analyzes awareness levels.
# Lack of Awareness of Government Schemes Among Farmers and Its Impact

## 📌 Project Overview

This project studies the lack of awareness among farmers about government schemes and the impact of this information gap. It focuses on identifying communication barriers and suggesting ways to improve the delivery of scheme-related information to farmers.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Study the awareness level of farmers about government schemes.
* Identify reasons for lack of awareness.
* Analyze the impact on farmers' income.
* Identify communication gaps between the government and farmers.
* Suggest methods to improve information delivery.
* Promote effective use of government schemes.

## ⚙️ Methodology

1. Literature Review
2. Identify Government Schemes
3. Survey Farmers
4. Analyze Data
5. Identify Problems
6. Suggest Solutions

### 🛠️ Tools Used

* Google Forms
* Farmer Interviews
* Government Reports
* Internet Research

## 📊 Expected Outcomes

* Better understanding of farmers' awareness.
* Identification of major communication barriers.
* Practical recommendations to increase awareness.
* Increased utilization of government schemes.
* Improved financial and social welfare of farmers.
